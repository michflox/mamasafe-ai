# MamaSafe AI — 2-Minute Demo Script

## Setup (before demo starts)
- Have browser tabs open: Dashboard, Assessment, Simulation
- Backend running at deployed URL or localhost:8000
- Frontend running at deployed URL or localhost:5173

## Demo Sequence

### 0:00-0:10 — Dashboard & Vision
"Hi, I'm [Name], a CRNA and founder of MamaSafe AI. Africa doesn't just need more doctors — frontline health workers need better tools. MamaSafe AI is an offline-first clinical decision support platform that turns WHO guidelines into actionable workflows."

Show dashboard:
- Active module: Maternal Emergencies (PPH)
- Roadmap: Pediatric, Sickle Cell, Malaria, Immunization
- Architecture cards: Gemini (AI), Rule Engine (safety), Offline First, Audit Trail

### 0:10-0:25 — Simulation Mode
"Before I show you the live workflow, let me demonstrate with a synthetic case — this is how clinicians train with MamaSafe."

Click Simulation → Run Case 001 (Severe PPH — Rural Secondary Facility)
- Show: CRITICAL risk, teaching points
- Point out: "This case has no blood bank, no operating theatre, no obstetrician. The system adapts escalation accordingly."

### 0:25-0:50 — Live Assessment
"Now let's walk through a real assessment. A 29-year-old woman, 20 minutes postpartum, bleeding heavily."

Navigate to Assessment:
- Show pre-filled demo data: HR 124, BP 88/52, EBL 1100mL, boggy uterus
- Show facility resources: No blood bank, no OR, no obstetrician — typical rural Nigeria
- Click "Run PPH Assessment"

### 0:50-1:20 — Results: The Deterministic Engine
"This is where MamaSafe AI is different from a chatbot."

Point out:
1. **DETERMINISTIC RULE ENGINE OUTPUT** banner — "This is not an LLM guessing. These are validated WHO rules."
2. Red flags: Shock index 1.41, severe PPH, hypotension, atonic uterus
3. Action checklist (prioritized 1-10):
   - Item 1: Call for help
   - Item 4: Oxytocin 10 IU with weight-based calculation shown
   - Item 5: Tranexamic acid 1g IV with WOMAN trial rationale
   - Item 6: Fluid resuscitation with 3:1 rule calculation
   - Item 8: Second-line uterotonics with contraindication warnings
   - Item 9: Escalation — "URGENT TRANSFER to facility with obstetrician, OR, anesthesia, blood bank"
4. Click "Confirm Action" on item 1 — green checkmark appears

### 1:20-1:35 — SBAR & Referral
"Communication saves lives in emergencies."

Click "Generate SBAR":
- Show structured handoff: Situation, Background, Assessment, Recommendation
- "This is generated by Gemini from verified structured data — not hallucinated."

Click "Generate Referral":
- Show referral note with urgency, transport recommendation, blood availability

### 1:35-1:50 — Offline Mode
"Most African facilities have intermittent internet. MamaSafe works offline."

Disconnect WiFi or stop backend:
- Show "Offline" badge appears in header
- Cached protocols still accessible
- Calculations still work locally
- "The clinical rule engine doesn't need cloud. Only Gemini features pause."

### 1:50-2:00 — Closing
"MamaSafe AI doesn't replace African healthcare workers. It multiplies their capability. One excellent PPH workflow today. A continent-scale platform tomorrow. Thank you."

## Key Talking Points
- Founder-market fit: I'm a CRNA who has managed hemorrhagic shock
- Safety-first: Deterministic rules, not LLM guesses, for clinical decisions
- African context: Resource-aware escalation, offline mode, low bandwidth
- Google AI: Gemini for NLP/education, Gemma for edge, Vertex for RAG
- Regulatory ready: Audit trails, source attribution, human confirmation
