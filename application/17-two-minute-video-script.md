# 17. Two-Minute Application Video Script

**Format:** founder to camera + live product footage (simulation mode, synthetic cases)
**Runtime:** ~2:00. **Tone:** calm, clinical, conviction — no hype.

---

**[0:00–0:15] — Cold open: product footage.**
Screen shows the PPH case: HR 124, BP 88/52, shock index flashing 1.4 — CRITICAL.

**Founder (voice-over):**
> "Every two minutes, a woman dies giving life. Sub-Saharan Africa carries seventy percent of those deaths. Nigeria alone lost an estimated eighty-two thousand mothers in 2020. The leading cause is hemorrhage — and it is treatable."

**[0:15–0:35] — Founder on camera, clinical setting.**
> "I'm a nurse anesthetist. I've managed hemorrhage with my own hands. And I'll tell you what saves people in a crisis: not genius — systems. Checklists. Correct doses. Knowing exactly when to escalate. The World Health Organization has published that knowledge. The problem is delivering it to a primary-health center in rural Nigeria at two in the morning — where there's one midwife, a patchy signal, and no time."

**[0:35–1:05] — Product footage: intake → pathway.**
> "This is MamaSafe AI. A frontline worker speaks what she sees — the AI structures it, she confirms every value. A deterministic rule engine — not the language model — computes the shock index, the doses, the contraindications, every one cited to WHO guidelines. There's the tranexamic-acid clock: three hours, because given early it cuts bleeding deaths by a third. Watch — she flags possible hypertension and ergometrine is instantly contraindicated."

**[1:05–1:25] — The offline moment. Toggle airplane mode on camera; the product keeps running.**
> "Now the part that matters most. No signal. Nothing stops. The pathways, the calculations, the escalation logic, the documentation — all offline. When the network returns, Google's Gemini makes it richer. Offline, it never abandons her."

**[1:25–1:45] — Handoff generation + audit trail reveal.**
> "When she needs to transfer, one tap builds the referral note — a complete SBAR handoff, read back aloud for confirmation. And behind everything: a tamper-evident audit trail. Every value, every rule, every citation, every human confirmation — provable."

**[1:45–2:00] — Founder on camera, close.**
> "MamaSafe AI doesn't replace Africa's health workers — it multiplies them. It's built on Google's AI: Gemini for language, Gemma for the edge, Vertex for evaluation. We're in development, simulation-only, and honest about it. One workflow — postpartum hemorrhage — done right. Then the platform. I'm asking the Africa Applied AI Lab to help us put this in the hands of the people who need it most."

**[End card]:** MamaSafe AI — *Offline clinical emergency copilot for Africa's frontline health workers.* Development-stage · simulation demo · synthetic data only.

---

## Production notes

- Show, don't tell: at least 70% of runtime is live product footage; the airplane-mode toggle must be a real, visible gesture.
- Burn a permanent "SIMULATION — SYNTHETIC DATA" watermark into all product footage.
- Subtitles in English; key statistics appear as lower-thirds with source attributions (UN MMEIG 2023; WHO 2025; WOMAN trial, Lancet 2017).
- No music swell over the mortality statistics; silence is stronger and more respectful.
