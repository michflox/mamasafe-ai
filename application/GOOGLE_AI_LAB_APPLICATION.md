# MamaSafe AI — Google Africa Applied AI Lab Application (Master Document)

**Applicant product:** MamaSafe AI — built on the AnesthesiaOS Africa platform
**Application target:** Google Africa Applied AI Lab, 2026 cycle
**Tagline:** *Offline clinical emergency copilot for Africa's frontline health workers.*
**Status:** Development-stage product with a working postpartum hemorrhage (PPH) demonstration workflow in simulation mode. No regulatory clearance is claimed anywhere in this application.

---

## One-sentence company description (final, canonical)

> **MamaSafe AI is an offline-first, multilingual clinical workflow and emergency-escalation copilot that puts verified, source-linked emergency protocols in the hands of Africa's frontline health workers — AI that assists, never replaces, the clinician.**

---

## The core narrative (used consistently across all 23 assets)

Africa does not only face a shortage of clinicians. Frontline health workers frequently work under severe **time, information, staffing, referral, and connectivity** constraints. MamaSafe AI turns verified medical knowledge into offline, actionable clinical workflows that help existing healthcare workers recognize emergencies earlier, execute critical protocols more consistently, and communicate referrals more effectively. The AI **multiplies** the capabilities of existing health workers. It does not replace them, and it never issues commands — it makes recommendations that a human clinician must review and confirm before any consequential action.

## Honesty statement (read this before evaluating)

- **Built (demonstrable today):** the postpartum hemorrhage end-to-end workflow in simulation mode — intake → shock index and risk categorization → red flags and missing-information detection → prioritized PPH action checklist → weight-based medication and fluid calculations from the deterministic rule engine → escalation thresholds and referral recommendation → structured referral note and SBAR handoff → human-confirmation logging → tamper-evident audit trail → offline operation — using synthetic cases only.
- **Designed, in active build:** Gemini live integration through the AI gateway (the demo runs on a mandatory offline, rule-derived fallback so it works with no API key and no network); severe preeclampsia/eclampsia, maternal sepsis, and newborn resuscitation modules (architecture stubs only); multilingual clinician/patient string separation.
- **Roadmap only:** Gemma on-device edge inference, Vertex AI evaluation/guardrails pipeline, Google Maps referral routing, pediatric/sickle-cell/malaria/immunization modules.
- **Never claimed:** FDA clearance, CE marking, NAFDAC or any clinical approval, mortality reduction, use on real patients. The product is clinical decision **support**: recommendations, never commands, with mandatory human confirmation before any consequential action.

## How the 23 assets hang together

The problem statement (02) establishes the constraint gap — not just a clinician shortage. The product description (03) and technical architecture (12) show the answer is already running, not promised. The safety strategy (09) is the responsible-AI differentiator: a deterministic clinical rule engine owns every dose, threshold, contraindication, and escalation criterion, while Gemini/Gemma are confined to language, retrieval, explanation, documentation, and simulation. Google AI integration (07) shows why this product is a natural fit for Gemini (intake structuring, grounded Q&A, handoff, translation, simulation), Gemma (offline edge inference), Vertex AI (evaluation, guardrails, monitoring), and later Maps (referral routing). The Africa impact thesis (06) ties the multiplier narrative to SDG 3.1 and the WHO PPH Roadmap (2024). Business model (10) and roadmap (11) show commercial and technical scale. Pilot and research proposals (14, 15) show scientific discipline. Founder story and fit (04, 05) show why this founder — a CRNA/anesthesia clinician who has managed hemorrhage and airway emergencies firsthand and who built the safety-first AnesthesiaOS platform lineage — is the right person to build it.
