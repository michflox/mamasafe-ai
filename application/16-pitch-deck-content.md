# 16. Pitch-Deck Content

Slide-by-slide content for a 12-slide application deck. (Design guidance: low-saturation, warm palette, generous whitespace, photography of real frontline settings with dignity, no stock-photo clichés.)

---

**Slide 1 — Title**
MamaSafe AI — *Offline clinical emergency copilot for Africa's frontline health workers.*
Built on the AnesthesiaOS Africa platform. Google Africa Applied AI Lab application, 2026.
Footer: Development-stage · simulation demo · synthetic data only.

**Slide 2 — The problem, in numbers**
- Every ~2 minutes, a woman dies of pregnancy/childbirth causes — ~260,000 deaths in 2023 [WHO, 2025].
- Sub-Saharan Africa: ~70% of maternal deaths; MMR 545/100,000 (2020) [UN MMEIG, 2023].
- Nigeria: ~82,000 maternal deaths in 2020 — 28.5% of the world total; MMR 1,047/100,000 [UN MMEIG, 2023].
- Hemorrhage is the #1 cause: ~27% of maternal deaths [WHO systematic analysis, Lancet GH 2025].

**Slide 3 — The deeper problem: five constraints**
Not only a clinician shortage — the workers who exist fight: **time, information, staffing, referral, connectivity.** WHO projects ~10M health workers short by 2030 [WHO, 2023]. Knowledge exists; delivery fails at the bedside at 2 a.m.

**Slide 4 — The product**
MamaSafe AI turns verified guidelines into **offline, executable clinical workflows**. First workflow, done to excellence: postpartum hemorrhage — recognition → protocol → escalation → handoff → audit. AI assists, never replaces; humans confirm every consequential action.

**Slide 5 — The demo (screenshots)**
Three-panel screenshot strip: (1) shock index 1.4 = CRITICAL with citation; (2) PPH checklist with live TXA 3-hour countdown + contraindication flip; (3) generated SBAR handoff. Caption: "Running today. Simulation mode. Works offline."

**Slide 6 — The safety architecture (the differentiator)**
Diagram: deterministic rule engine (doses, thresholds, contraindications — cited to WHO/FIGO/FMOH) as the spine; LLM confined to language. RULE-BASED vs AI-GENERATED stamps. Tamper-evident audit trail. Adversarial eval suite gating every update. Honest posture: decision support, development-stage, no clearance claims.

**Slide 7 — The offline moment**
Photo of a rural PHC + screenshot with airplane-mode icon. "We kill the network live on camera. The product doesn't notice." Offline-first: cached pathways, local storage, deferred sync, rule-derived offline explanations, older-Android support.

**Slide 8 — Why Google AI**
- **Gemini:** intake structuring, grounded Q&A with citations, handoff drafting, translation, simulation.
- **Gemma:** offline edge inference for zero-connectivity PHCs (roadmap).
- **Vertex AI:** evaluation, guardrails, monitoring, version control.
- **Maps (later):** referral routing where data allow.
"Google AI is the language layer of a product whose clinical spine is deterministic."

**Slide 9 — Founder-market fit**
CRNA/anesthesia clinician: hemorrhage resuscitation, medication safety, emergency airway — daily professional substance. Builder of safety-first AnesthesiaOS. Researcher: every claim cited. Mission: years of commitment to Nigerian/African healthcare. "I've managed this emergency with my own hands."

**Slide 10 — Impact thesis & evidence plan**
Multiplier model: raise the capability of existing workers. SDG 3.1 + WHO PPH Roadmap (2024) alignment. Staged evidence: simulation metrics now → shadow-mode concordance → pilot workflow outcomes. **We do not claim mortality reduction without adequate evidence.**

**Slide 11 — Business model**
Facility subscriptions · government licensing · NGO deployment · training licenses · (later) API. Non-negotiable: emergency functionality stays affordable; no per-use metering of emergencies; no data monetization. Expansion: maternal suite → pediatrics → sickle cell → malaria → immunization → Africa Clinical Copilot.

**Slide 12 — The ask**
Admission to the Google Africa Applied AI Lab: Gemini/Gemma multilingual + edge mentorship; Vertex AI evaluation partnership; credibility with ministries and NGOs. Close with the one-liner:
**"MamaSafe AI puts verified emergency knowledge in the hands of the health workers Africa already has — offline, in their language, with the clinician always in command."**
